## How the nets model the functions

1-D nets: kinks (ReLU) or unit centres (sigmoid, tanh) inside the box, stacked units counted once, against the curvature-optimal knots for the same count (density √|f″|). *gap*: mean distance between the sorted lists, as a share of the box. *max error at*: where the net is furthest from the true function.
